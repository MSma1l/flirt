## 3. What data we process

### 3.1. Account and authentication data

- the internal account identifier, creation date and time of last activity;
- your e-mail address and password (stored only as an Argon2 cryptographic hash — we do not know your password), if you sign in with e-mail;
- when signing in via Telegram: your Telegram user ID (stored as the account identity); at sign-in Telegram also sends us your first name, username, language and Telegram profile photo URL, used only for identification and pre-filling; for these accounts a technical e-mail address is generated, which does not belong to you and is not used for communication;
- your phone number, if you sign in with an SMS code (feature currently disabled);
- your Google or Apple identifier, if you sign in through those services (features currently disabled);
- account status: role, any suspension and its reason, deletion request and its date.

### 3.2. Profile data

- display name, date of birth (other users only see your age), gender, height;
- city, optionally street, nationality, spoken languages;
- approximate geographic coordinates, derived from your city/street by geocoding or from your device, used to calculate distance; other users only see the approximate distance, never coordinates or street;
- bio (up to 500 characters), interests, dating status (what you are looking for);
- answers to the humour test and the resulting humour profile;
- profile photos (between 2 and 9) and the "verified" badge.

### 3.3. Settings and preferences

- search preferences: genders you are interested in, age range, search radius, region;
- notification preferences, theme, hide-profile option;
- the option to enable AI features (off by default).

### 3.4. Face verification (optional)

If you choose to verify your profile, you take a selfie that is automatically compared with your profile photos. **The selfie is not stored**: it is processed only for the duration of the comparison. We keep only the result (profile verified or not). This processing takes place only with your separate, explicit consent (see section 5).

### 3.5. Social activity and content

- likes, passes, super-likes (including any message sent along with them), matches, favourites and blocks;
- chat messages, reactions, read status and attached files (photos, videos, voice messages), with their size, duration and resolution;
- stories: photos or videos with captions;
- reports you submit or that concern you (category, note, reference to the conversation).

### 3.6. Events, tickets and payments

- event attendance ("going"), Flirt Passport stamps, loyalty tier, discounts and invitations used;
- your ticket (QR code) and the time it was scanned at the entrance;
- ticket orders paid by bank transfer or MIA instant payment: full name, phone, e-mail, your message, number of tickets, amount, payment reference, image of the payment proof (which may contain bank details), order status, administrator notes and decision times;
- subscriptions and in-app purchases in the mobile app (plan, transaction identifiers, expiry date), received from the App Store or Google Play.

We do not receive or store your bank card details. Payments are made through your bank or the app store.

### 3.7. Devices and notifications

- push notification token and platform type (when push notifications are enabled);
- notification messages sent through the Telegram bot @flrt_party_bot.

### 3.8. Technical data

- IP address, user agent (browser/app type), request identifier, access and application logs;
- temporary counters used to limit abuse (rate limiting).

### 3.9. AI features (optional)

If you enable AI features in Settings: message suggestions use the last few messages of the conversation, truncated, without names and without identifiers; the "chemistry score" uses profile data of both users (interests, status, languages and similar).

### 3.10. Records

- consent records: the accepted document, version, date and time, IP address and user agent;
- a log of administrative actions taken by our team.

## 5. Special categories of data

**Sexual orientation.** Your gender, the genders you are interested in and your dating status may indirectly reveal your sexual orientation, which is a special category of data. We process this data only on the basis of your explicit consent (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)), given when you enter the app, and only to recommend suitable profiles to you.

**Biometric data.** Face verification compares the facial features in your selfie with your profile photos. This is processing of biometric data for identification purposes, carried out only with your separate, explicit consent. The selfie is not stored. Refusing has no consequences other than your profile not receiving the "verified" badge.

**Other sensitive data.** We do not ask for data about health, religion, political opinions, ethnic origin or criminal records. Please do not include such information in your bio, stories or messages. If you do, it becomes visible to the people it is addressed to.

## 6. Automated decision-making and profiling

We use the following automated processing:

- **Recommendation order:** an algorithm calculates a compatibility score based on interests, dating status, the humour test, distance, languages and in-app activity, and profiles are shown in the order of this score, within your preferences.
- **Automatic hiding:** an account reported by several different users may be hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it.
- **Automatic photo moderation:** uploaded photos are automatically checked for nudity or prohibited content and may be rejected.
- **Contact detail masking:** phone numbers and other contact details in messages are masked automatically to protect against scams.

This processing does not produce legal effects concerning you. Permanent suspension of an account is decided by a human moderator. You may at any time request human intervention, express your point of view and contest an automated decision by writing to [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}). You may object to the profiling used to order recommendations; in that case recommendations may become less relevant.

## 7. Minimum age

If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we suspend it and delete the data. You can report such cases to [{{contact_email}}](mailto:{{contact_email}}).

## 8. Who we share data with

**Other users.** Your public profile is visible to other users: name, age, photos, city, approximate distance, interests, bio, "verified" badge and stories. Messages are visible only to the participants of the conversation. We never display your e-mail, phone, date of birth, street or coordinates.

**Processors (providers processing data on our behalf):**

- the hosting provider (virtual server in a data centre), where our servers run and media files are stored;
- Telegram (Telegram FZ-LLC / Telegram Messenger Inc.) — authentication, running the Mini App and bot notifications;
- OpenRouter, Inc. and the AI model provider (Anthropic) — AI features and automatic photo moderation, **when enabled**;

- Amazon Web Services — face verification (Rekognition) and file storage (S3), **when enabled**;
- Apple and Google — in-app purchases in the mobile app and push notifications, **when enabled**;
- Expo and Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivery of push notifications, **when enabled**;
- Twilio — sending SMS codes, **when phone sign-in is enabled**.

Providers process data only on our instructions and are contractually bound to protect it.

**Other recipients.** Banks and payment institutions through which you pay (they act as independent controllers). Public authorities, courts and law enforcement bodies, where the law requires it or to protect a person's life and safety. Our legal and accounting advisers, under a duty of confidentiality.

**We do not sell data** and do not pass it to advertising networks or data brokers.

## 10. How long we keep data

- **Account and profile:** as long as the account is active.
- **After a deletion request:** the profile is hidden immediately and all sessions are signed out. After a grace period of **{{grace_days}} days**, during which you can cancel the request, the following are automatically and permanently deleted or anonymised: profile, photos, stories, conversations and messages, likes, matches, favourites, blocks, settings, sessions, notification devices, ticket, event attendance, Flirt Passport stamps and subscription, including the related files (photos, stories, chat attachments). Ticket orders are not deleted but anonymised (see below).
- **Backups:** databases and media files are backed up periodically and backups are kept for **{{backup_days}} days**; deleted data disappears from backups within at most {{backup_days}} days.
- **Moderation reports:** retained after account deletion, linked to the anonymised account, to prevent abuse and to defend legal claims.
- **Stories:** visible to others for 24 hours, then archived in your account until the account is deleted; you can delete them at any time.
- **Messages:** until your account or your conversation partner's account is deleted.
- **Verification selfie:** not stored; only the verification result is kept.
- **Ticket orders:** when the account is deleted, orders are anonymised: name, phone, e-mail, your messages, notes and the payment description are erased. The amount, currency, number of tickets, payment reference, event, dates and order status are kept as accounting records, as required by Law No. 287/2017 on accounting and financial reporting; they no longer identify you.
- **Payment proofs (receipt images):** kept for **{{proof_retention_years}} years** from the order date, as supporting accounting documents, then automatically deleted. Proofs of rejected or cancelled orders are not accounting documents: they are deleted **{{rejected_proof_days}} days** after the decision, or immediately when the account is deleted.
- **Consent records:** for the life of the account; after deletion they are kept as evidence, without the IP address and user agent.
- **Technical logs:** access logs record the IP address only in truncated form (for example 1.2.3.0) and do not contain request parameters. Logs are rotated automatically by size and deleted after at most **{{log_retention_days}} days**; error logs (for example blocked attack attempts) may contain the full IP address and are subject to the same limit.
- **Abuse-limiting counters:** from a few minutes to a few hours.
- **Requests to the AI provider:** processed by the provider only to generate the response; we do not store them, except for short-lived temporary caching.

## 11. Your rights

## 13. Data security

- all traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS);
- passwords are stored only as Argon2 hashes;
- Telegram sign-in is verified cryptographically (signature of the launch data), with replay protection;
- sessions with short-lived access tokens, and revocation of all sessions on a deletion request;
- request rate limiting against attacks and abuse;
- validation of uploaded files (type, size, content);
- access to production data limited to authorised persons, with logging of administrative actions;
- periodic backups with a limited retention period.

No system is completely secure. In the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to you, we will notify the CNPDCP and, where required, you, within the time limits set by law.
