## 1. General consent (required to use FLIRT)

By ticking the corresponding box, I declare that:

- I am at least 18 years old;
- I have read the FLIRT Privacy Policy and Terms of Use;
- I agree to the processing of my personal data for operating my account, showing my profile and recommending suitable profiles, conversations and event participation, under the conditions described in the Privacy Policy, in accordance with Law No. 195/2024;
- I give my **explicit consent** (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)) for my gender, the genders I am interested in and my dating status — which may reveal my sexual orientation — to be processed for the purpose of recommending profiles.

## 2. Separate consent for face verification (optional)

If I tick the separate box for face verification, I agree that:

- the selfie I take in the app is automatically compared with my profile photos, to confirm that the profile belongs to me and to receive the "verified" badge;
- the comparison is carried out by the facial recognition service provider (Amazon Web Services Rekognition, when the service is enabled), acting as the controller's processor.

This is processing of biometric data, based on my explicit consent (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)). **The selfie is not stored** — only the verification result is kept. This consent is optional: refusing has no consequences other than the profile not receiving the "verified" badge.

## 3. Withdrawing consent

- You can withdraw your consent to face verification at any time in the app (Settings) or by e-mail.
- The general consent is necessary for the service to work; you can withdraw it by deleting your account (Settings → "Delete account") or by writing to us.
- Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
